[Claude's working brief] — flood simulation (sim.js)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each season:
1. Tide: flood-fill from the river. A land block floods if its height (plus
   dike or mangrove protection) is below the tide. The tide also travels up the
   canal unless the canal mouth has a tide gate.
2. Rain: add rain depth x runoff factor x 3 (streets concentrate runoff).
   Runoff: apartments 0.95, homes 0.85, empty lot 0.6, park 0.25, wetland 0.15.
3. Flow: 14 passes in which water moves from each block to lower neighbouring
   water surfaces. Riverbank blocks without a dike drain to the river when the
   water is above the tide; the canal mouth drains down to the tide level (or to
   zero if gated and pumped). A retention pond's floor sits 0.9 m lower, so it
   collects water.
4. Drains: remove 0.28 m from their block and 0.12 m from neighbours, scaled by
   how well the outlets can empty (100% at low tide or with a gate, 50% or 20%
   at higher tides).
5. Damage: a block with more than 15 cm of water is flooded; flooded homes cost
   repairs and lower happiness; dry blocks pay full tax.
After the season the land sinks 4 cm unless the clean-water policy is in place.
Forecasts are seeded and shown in advance; the actual season varies by about 8%.

[Claude's working brief] — interface (main.js, style.css)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Board: CSS grid of buttons, one per block, each with SVG art, a water layer
  whose opacity follows the flood depth, a red dashed outline for blocks that
  flooded last season, and an optional height label.
- Hover or keyboard focus shows a tooltip: name in English and Vietnamese,
  height above the river, residents, defences, last season's flooding, and
  whether the selected tool can be built there (with the reason if not).
- Season sequence: rain canvas and sound -> tide gauge rises -> water layers fade
  in -> report dialog with flooded share, tax, repairs, happiness, causes, fact.
- Undo stack for builds within the current season.
- Keyboard: 1–9 tools, H heights, Z undo. Visible focus outlines. Respect
  prefers-reduced-motion. Responsive down to phone width.
