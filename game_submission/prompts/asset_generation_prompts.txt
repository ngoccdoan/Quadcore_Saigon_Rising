[Claude's working brief] — map tiles (js/art.js)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Draw top-down 64x64 SVG tiles, flat colours, no outlines except where they carry
meaning:
- Homes: four tile roofs in varied terracotta shades on a grey lane.
- Apartments: one tall grey-blue block with a grid of windows.
- Market: red-and-white striped awning with fruit dots at the front.
- School: yellow building with a red flag carrying a yellow star.
- Wetland: green ground, a shallow pool, scattered reeds.
- Empty lot: sandy ground with a dashed boundary and a few weeds.
- Rain park: lawn, path and round trees.
- Retention pond: rounded blue basin with a concrete rim.
- Mangroves: dark canopies with visible stilt roots over water.
- River and canal: blue water with ripple lines; canal has stone banks.
Overlays, drawn on top of any tile:
- Drains: dark grate in the corner.
- Dike: hatched concrete wall on the side facing the river.
- Tide gate: yellow gate panel at the canal mouth.
- Raised building: one blue chevron per level (max two).
Use a deterministic hash of the row and column so roofs and reeds vary between
blocks but stay the same every time the map redraws.

[Claude's working brief] — sound (js/audio.js)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Synthesised with the Web Audio API, no audio files:
- click: short triangle-wave blip
- build: two rising triangle notes
- error: low falling square tone
- rain: looping high-passed white noise, louder for heavier forecasts
- thunder: low-passed noise burst for storm seasons
- good / bad season: rising or falling three-note phrase
Respect a mute toggle on every screen.
