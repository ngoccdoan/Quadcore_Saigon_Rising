[Claude's working brief] — balance testing
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Simulate hundreds of games in Node with different strategies to make sure doing
nothing loses and smart planning wins."

Strategies: do nothing / sell everything to developers / drains only / a sensible
plan (tide gate first, then clean water, drains on the lowest homes, ponds,
raise flooded homes, mangroves). 300 seeded games each.

Round 1 result: doing nothing failed every game, but the first season flooded
11 of 27 home blocks before a new player could react.
Refinement: lower the opening tide (1.10 m -> 1.00 m base) and let it climb
faster over time (+2.5 cm -> +3.5 cm per round).

Round 2 result: the sensible plan earned mostly A grades — too easy once money
piled up.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Claude's working brief] — economy
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Playtest screenshots showed ₫534B unspent by 2032 and ₫770B at the end, so
spending choices stopped mattering.
Refinement 1: cut taxes (homes 7 -> 4, apartments 13 -> 8, market 16 -> 10).
  Result: too tight — the sensible plan was voted out in 137 of 300 games.
Refinement 2: middle ground (homes 5, apartments 10, market 12).
  Result: careless strategies always fail; the sensible plan mostly earns B
  (some A and C) and finishes with about ₫46B left.

[Claude's working brief] — visual fixes from screenshots
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The title's "water" was a flat blue box over the letters. Fixed by layering a
  second copy of the title filled with a water gradient and clipping it, so the
  letters themselves look half submerged; then extended the clip box so the
  descender of the "g" is covered too.
- The forecast label on the tide gauge overlapped the scale numbers; moved it
  below the dashed line.
- The "pick a tool" hint stayed visible during the season; hide it when the
  season starts.
- Shallow floodwater was too faint to read; raised the water layer's minimum
  opacity.
- Yellow "you can build here" outlines showed during the season; hide them while
  the season plays.
- On phones the tool tray wrapped into three rows and covered half the screen;
  made it a single row that scrolls sideways.
