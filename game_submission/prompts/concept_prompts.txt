[Claude's working brief] — game concept
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Title: Saigon Rising. Player is the planner of a fictional riverside district,
  Bến Nước, on the Saigon River.
- 12 rounds, one per two-year rainy season, 2026 to 2050.
- Each round: read a forecast (rain in mm, peak tide in m) -> spend a budget on
  defences -> the season plays out (rain, tide, water flowing downhill) -> report.
- The mechanics must each teach a real cause of flooding in HCMC:
    * high tides stop drains from emptying (tide gate at the canal mouth fixes it)
    * groundwater pumping makes the land sink every season (clean-water policy)
    * concrete sheds rain; wetlands, parks and ponds absorb it
    * dikes keep the river out but trap rain behind them
    * mangroves buffer the riverbank
- Temptation mechanic: "Sell to developer" pays now and floods later.
- Lose if residents' happiness reaches 0; final grade A–F from the share of
  residents kept dry.
- Show a short, sourced real-world fact in every season report.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Claude's working brief] — visual direction
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Draw the look from the subject itself: painted red-and-white flood-depth staffs,
  blue Saigon street signs, tile roofs, rain on concrete.
- Palette: rain-washed concrete grey, street-sign blue for actions, flood-staff
  red only for danger, water blue, vegetation green.
- Typeface: Be Vietnam Pro (designed for Vietnamese diacritics).
- One memorable element: a live tide-gauge staff beside the map that the water
  climbs each season, and a title whose letters are half under water.
- Checked against generic AI-design defaults (cream + serif, dark + neon,
  identical cards) and rejected them in favour of the subject-specific look.
