QuậnBến Nước

How to play

You are the planner for Bến Nước, an imaginary district on the bank of the Saigon River. Every two years the rainy season arrives. Your job is to keep people's homes out of the water.

  1. Read the forecast. It shows how much rain is coming and how high the tide will climb up the gauge.
  2. Build. Pick a tool from the tray, then click a block on the map. Hover over any block to see how high it sits above the river.
  3. Start the season. Rain falls, the tide pushes in, and water runs downhill. Blocks under more than 15 cm of water are flooded.
  4. Read the report. Flooded homes cost repairs and make residents unhappy. If happiness hits zero, you are replaced.

Things to know

  • Drains can only empty when the tide is low. A tide gate at the canal mouth fixes that.
  • The ground sinks every season while people pump groundwater — until you pay for clean piped water.
  • Concrete sheds rain. Wetlands, parks and ponds soak it up. Selling land to developers pays now and floods later.
  • Dikes keep the river out, but they also keep rainwater in.
